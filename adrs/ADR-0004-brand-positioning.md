# ADR-0004: Brand Positioning — Apple Simplicity × Palantir Depth

**Status:** Accepted

## Context

Aegis serves a serious purpose — capital allocation — for a non-professional customer. Two failure modes threaten the product. It can be so complex that the individual investor is overwhelmed and distrusts it, or so simplistic that it hides the rigor required to earn trust. The brand must resolve this tension deliberately rather than by accident.

The founder frames the target as the combination of two references: the calm, minimal, confidence-inspiring surface associated with Apple, and the rigorous, deep, evidence-rich substance associated with Palantir.

## Decision

Aegis positions itself as **Apple-level simplicity on the surface, Palantir-level depth beneath it**.

The surface is minimal, calm, and immediately understandable. The depth — evidence, reasoning, provenance, and auditability — is always present and always reachable, never removed and never forced.

## Consequences

- Interfaces default to clarity and restraint; complexity is progressively disclosed, not front-loaded.
- Every simplification on the surface must be backed by real depth beneath it; simplicity is never achieved by discarding rigor.
- Explanations are layered: a plain answer first, with full evidence and reasoning one step away.
- Design and engineering both carry responsibility for this balance; neither a beautiful shell over shallow logic nor deep logic behind a hostile interface is acceptable.
