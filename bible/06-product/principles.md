# Product Design Principles

These principles govern every product decision in Aegis. They are not aspirations; they are constraints. When a feature request, a growth tactic, or a design shortcut conflicts with one of them, the principle wins. Aegis exists to improve the quality of an investor's capital-allocation decisions. Anything that degrades decision quality in exchange for engagement, revenue, or convenience is out of scope by definition.

## 1. Progressive disclosure of depth

Aegis is built on Adaptive Quality Investing, which is inherently deep. But depth presented all at once is noise. Every surface begins with the single most decision-relevant fact and lets the investor descend, deliberately, toward the full evidentiary basis beneath it.

A Holding first shows its Thesis status in one line. One interaction reveals the Investment Case supporting it. A further step exposes the underlying Evidence, Observations, and their sources. The investor controls the descent; the system never forces the full depth into view, and never hides it. Depth is always one honest step away, never buried and never dumped.

This is the operational meaning of "Apple simplicity × Palantir depth." Simplicity is the default state. Depth is the guaranteed availability.

## 2. Explainability by default

Aegis never asserts. It shows its work. Every Observation the system surfaces carries its source, its date, and its logical connection to the affected Thesis. Every status change on a Holding is traceable to the specific Evidence that produced it. Every ranking, flag, or prioritization exposes the reasoning that ordered it.

An investor must never encounter a conclusion they cannot interrogate. If a claim cannot be explained in terms the investor can inspect and challenge, the system does not make the claim. This holds for automated and human-authored content alike: confidence without a visible basis is prohibited.

Explainability is also a trust mechanism. An investor who can see why the system surfaced something can calibrate how much to weight it. Opaque authority produces either blind compliance or blanket dismissal — both are failures of decision quality.

## 3. No dark patterns, no engagement-bait

Aegis does not optimize for time-on-app, session count, notification open rates, or any proxy for attention. These metrics are actively adversarial to the investor's interest. A long-term investor who checks Aegis rarely and acts deliberately is a success, not a lapsed user.

Concretely, this forbids: manufactured urgency, streaks, badges, points, celebratory animation on trades, red-dot notification pressure, infinite feeds, loss-aversion framing designed to provoke action, and any nudge whose purpose is a behavior that benefits Aegis rather than the investor. Notifications exist only to surface a genuine change in the Evidence relevant to a Thesis. If nothing has changed, the investor hears nothing.

The absence of these patterns is not a marketing stance. It is a structural commitment: the product's incentives are aligned to decision quality, and any mechanic that trades on compulsion is a defect.

## 4. Respect the investor's time horizon

V1 serves long-term public-equity investors. The product's entire tempo is set to that horizon. Prices are context, not headlines. Daily volatility is not an event. The system does not present intraday movement as information demanding a response, because for this investor it almost never is.

Aegis distinguishes noise from signal on the investor's behalf and defends their attention accordingly. A Thesis is evaluated over the timeframe on which it can actually be judged. The product refuses to compress a multi-year hypothesis into a daily verdict. When Aegis does interrupt the investor, it is because something changed that bears on a Thesis they have explicitly formed — and the interruption arrives at the cadence the change warrants, not the cadence that maximizes contact.

## 5. Never suggest a Decision without its basis

Aegis does not issue buy/sell signals. It surfaces changes against a Thesis and frames them as confirm-or-challenge. When the product brings a possible Decision into view, it presents that Decision inseparably from the Thesis it affects and the Evidence that motivated it. A recommendation stripped of its reasoning is a bet dressed as advice, and Aegis does not make it.

Every Decision the investor records captures the reasoning at the moment of choice. This is not bureaucratic friction; it is the raw material of Learning Events, Knowledge, and the Behavior Profile that compounds over time. A Decision without recorded reasoning teaches nothing later. The product therefore treats the reasoning as the primary artifact and the transaction as its consequence.

## 6. The investor is the decision-maker

Aegis is an intelligence layer, not an agent acting on the investor's behalf. It orders evidence, frames questions, and preserves the record. It never executes a Decision the investor did not make, never quietly re-weights a Thesis the investor authored, and never substitutes its judgment for theirs. Authority over capital remains, at every step, with the person allocating it.

## Applying the principles

When these principles tension against each other — depth against simplicity, thoroughness against respect for time — resolve toward the investor's long-term decision quality. That is the invariant. Every other value in this volume is downstream of it.
