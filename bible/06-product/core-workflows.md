# Core Workflows

These are the primary paths an investor travels through Aegis. Each is described as it is actually experienced, step by step, and each embodies the product principles: progressive disclosure, explainability, respect for the investor's horizon, and never a Decision without its basis. Together they constitute the core product loop.

## Workflow 1 — Connecting IBKR and reviewing existing Holdings

**Trigger:** A new investor, or an existing one adding an account.

1. **Connect.** The investor initiates the Interactive Brokers connection from onboarding or from Mission & Settings. Aegis authenticates via IBKR and establishes read access to positions. The connection's scope and permissions are shown plainly; nothing is executed on the investor's behalf.

2. **Sync.** The **Portfolio** and its **Holdings** populate. Each position becomes a Holding linked to a **Company**. The sync is presented as state, not as a performance dashboard — the investor sees what they own, not a celebration of gains or an alarm over losses.

3. **Confront the implicit.** Aegis presents every Holding with its thesis status, and for existing portfolios this is almost always the same at first: *no explicit Thesis on record*. This is the pivotal moment. The product makes visible that the investor is holding capital in positions whose rationale exists only implicitly. Each un-thesised Holding is framed as an open task — an invitation, never a reprimand.

4. **Prioritize.** Aegis orders the Holdings so the investor can begin where it matters most — typically the largest positions, or those where public Evidence already suggests the original rationale may be under pressure. The investor is not asked to document everything at once; the horizon-respecting design lets them build their theses over weeks.

5. **Enter the loop.** Selecting any Holding moves the investor into the Company workspace and into Workflow 2. Reviewing Holdings is not an end state; it is the on-ramp to making tacit bets explicit.

## Workflow 2 — Forming a new Investment Thesis for a Company

**Trigger:** A Holding without a Thesis, or a researched Company the investor is considering.

1. **Open the workspace.** The investor enters the **Company** workspace. Aegis presents what it already knows: current Evidence, recent Observations, identified Risks and Catalysts — the raw material for a Case, not a pre-written conclusion.

2. **State the hypothesis.** The investor writes the **Investment Thesis** in their own words: why this company, why now, why held. The product resists reducing this to a rating. A Thesis is a claim about the future that can later be judged.

3. **Build the Case.** Guided by the workspace — this scaffolding is what the First-Process Investor most needs — the investor structures the **Investment Case** beneath the Thesis: what has to be true for it to hold, and, critically, what would disprove it. Stating the disconfirming conditions up front is what later lets the system challenge the Thesis honestly rather than flatter it.

4. **Attach Evidence.** The investor connects supporting **Evidence** from the Library or imports their own research. Each piece is sourced and dated. The Case now stands on an inspectable basis rather than conviction alone.

5. **Name Risks and Catalysts.** The investor records the **Risks** that threaten the Thesis and the **Catalysts** that could resolve it. These become the specific things Aegis watches on the investor's behalf.

6. **Commit.** The Thesis is saved and bound to the Holding. From here Aegis tracks Evidence and Observations against it continuously — and stays silent until something genuinely bears on it.

## Workflow 3 — Responding to an Observation that challenges a Thesis

**Trigger:** Aegis detects an Observation materially relevant to an existing Thesis. This is the only category of event that earns an interruption.

1. **Surfaced, with basis.** The investor is notified — at a cadence matched to the change's significance, never on a manufactured schedule. The notification names the affected Holding and states, in one line, what changed. It never says "sell" or "buy."

2. **See the Observation in context.** Opening it, the investor sees the **Observation** with its source and date, and its explicit logical connection to the specific element of the **Investment Case** it bears on. Explainability is absolute here: the investor can trace exactly why Aegis judged this relevant.

3. **Confirm or challenge.** The product frames the question as it always does: does this Evidence *confirm* or *challenge* the Thesis? The investor can descend into the full Case, review adjacent Evidence, and reassess whether their disconfirming conditions have been triggered.

4. **Decide the response.** The investor concludes one of: the Thesis still holds and the Observation is noted; the Thesis is weakened and warrants adjustment; or the Thesis is broken and the position should change. Each conclusion leads into Workflow 4. Doing nothing, when nothing is warranted, is a legitimate and recorded outcome.

## Workflow 4 — Making and recording a Decision

**Trigger:** The investor decides to act on, or deliberately hold through, a challenged Thesis.

1. **Frame the Decision with its basis.** Aegis presents the pending **Decision** inseparably from the Thesis it affects and the Evidence that motivated it. There is no path to a Decision that hides its rationale.

2. **Record the reasoning.** Before anything else, the investor writes why. This is the primary artifact — the material that makes a Learning Event possible later. The transaction is treated as the reasoning's consequence, not the point.

3. **Act.** For a portfolio change, the investor executes through their IBKR account. Aegis records the Decision and links it to the reasoning, the Thesis, and the Evidence. The investor remains the actor; Aegis is the record and the intelligence, never the executor of an unmade choice.

4. **Close the loop.** The Decision enters the **Decision log**. As its outcome unfolds against the recorded reasoning, it becomes a **Learning Event**, feeding **Knowledge** and the **Behavior Profile**. The next cycle through the loop is framed by what this one taught. Over many cycles, the investor's judgment becomes visible, auditable, and improvable — which is the entire purpose of the product.
