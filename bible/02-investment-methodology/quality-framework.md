# The Quality Framework

The Quality Framework is the concrete instrument AQI uses to assess an investment. It decomposes "quality" into five independent dimensions, each with explicit criteria, so that an investor — and Aegis — can locate *where* an investment is strong or weak rather than issuing a single undifferentiated verdict. The dimensions are assessed separately and are never silently averaged into one score, because a fatal weakness in one dimension (an unfalsifiable Thesis, a fabricated Evidence base) cannot be redeemed by strength in another.

## Dimension 1 — Business Quality

Assesses the Company as a real-world economic entity. This dimension changes slowly and is largely independent of price.

Criteria:
- **Competitive position.** Is there a durable structural advantage (scale, network, switching costs, regulatory, brand, cost)? Is it widening or eroding? Naming the *source* of the moat is required; asserting a moat without a mechanism scores low.
- **Economics.** Return on incremental invested capital, gross and operating margin structure, and the cash-conversion quality of reported earnings. The question is whether growth *creates* value (returns above cost of capital) or merely consumes capital.
- **Resilience.** Behavior of the business through downturns, demand shocks, and input-cost swings. Concentration in customers, suppliers, or single products counts against resilience.
- **Market structure.** Size, growth, and rationality of the end market; intensity and conduct of competitors.

Assessment operates on the Company object and feeds Knowledge that persists across every Holding and Thesis referencing that Company.

## Dimension 2 — Management Quality

Assessed separately from the business because good businesses can be run by poor stewards and vice versa.

Criteria:
- **Capital allocation record.** History of reinvestment, acquisitions, buybacks, and dividends measured against the returns each generated. This is the single most predictive management criterion.
- **Alignment.** Insider ownership, compensation structure, and whether incentives reward durable value creation or short-term optics.
- **Candor.** Consistency between what management said would happen and what did; willingness to disclose bad news plainly. Prior statements are retained as Evidence and scored against outcomes over time.
- **Operational execution.** Delivery against stated operational targets, independent of market conditions.

## Dimension 3 — Thesis Quality

Assesses the Investment Thesis as an object of reasoning, independent of whether it is currently correct.

Criteria:
- **Falsifiability.** Does the Thesis state conditions that would prove it *wrong*? A Thesis with no invalidation conditions scores zero on this criterion regardless of eloquence.
- **Specificity.** Are the claims concrete enough to be tested against future Evidence (magnitudes, timeframes, mechanisms), rather than directional sentiment?
- **Load-bearing structure.** Does the Thesis identify the *few things that actually have to be true*, separating them from incidental positives? A Thesis resting on one critical assumption is fragile but honest; a Thesis resting on twelve equally-weighted points is usually unexamined.
- **Coherence with Capital Mission.** Does the Thesis's implied horizon and risk profile fit the investor's stated Capital Mission?

## Dimension 4 — Evidence Quality

Assesses the factual foundation of the Investment Case. Detailed rules live in the Evidence and Observation chapter; here they enter as scored criteria.

Criteria:
- **Sourcing.** Is each piece of Evidence traceable to a primary or reliable source, versus rumor, unattributed opinion, or circular market commentary?
- **Recency and validity window.** Is the Evidence current enough to bear on the Thesis, and is its natural expiry acknowledged?
- **Relevance.** Does the Evidence actually bear on a load-bearing element of the Thesis, or is it decorative?
- **Balance.** Does the Case include genuine disconfirming Evidence, or only confirming? An Investment Case with no disconfirming Evidence is treated as *incomplete*, not as strong — the absence signals that disconfirmation was not sought.

## Dimension 5 — Process Quality

Assesses the reasoning process that turns the prior four dimensions into a Decision. This is the dimension most within the investor's control and the one Aegis most directly instruments.

Criteria:
- **Grounding.** Does every Decision reference a documented Thesis and Investment Case? Ungrounded Decisions score zero regardless of outcome.
- **Bias check.** Were the investor's characteristic failure modes, as recorded in the Behavior Profile, explicitly considered? (e.g., is this a familiar over-sizing pattern, a familiar averaging-down-into-a-loser pattern?)
- **Disconfirmation discipline.** Was contrary Evidence actively sought and weighed, not merely acknowledged?
- **Policy adherence.** Were applicable Policy guardrails (position limits, concentration limits, cooling-off rules) honored, and were exceptions recorded with justification?
- **Consistency.** Is this Decision reasoned in a manner consistent with how comparable past Decisions were reasoned, or is the standard being bent to fit a desired conclusion?

## How the dimensions combine

The five dimensions produce a **quality profile**, not a scalar. Aegis renders each dimension explicitly and flags the binding constraint — the weakest load-bearing dimension. Two rules govern combination:

1. **Falsifiability and grounding are gating.** A Thesis that cannot be falsified, or a Decision not grounded in a Case, caps overall quality regardless of business or management strength. These are the criteria that make learning possible; without them, the position cannot feed the Learning Loop.

2. **Dimensions are tracked over time, not just at entry.** Every dimension is re-assessed as Evidence accumulates and Risks materialize. A Holding whose Business Quality is intact but whose Thesis Quality has degraded (because its load-bearing assumption was invalidated) is a *sell candidate*, and the framework is designed to surface exactly that divergence.

The Quality Framework is therefore both an entry filter and a monitoring instrument. It defines what "good" means precisely enough that an investor can be shown *which* kind of quality a Holding has and lacks — and precisely enough that Aegis can compute, track, and explain that assessment against the canonical objects.
