# The Investment Thesis Lifecycle

An Investment Thesis is the central reasoning object in AQI: the explicit, falsifiable belief about why a Company is a good investment and what must be true for it to succeed. Because AQI is *adaptive*, a Thesis is never static — it moves through a defined lifecycle, and every transition is triggered by a specific, recordable event. This chapter defines the states, the transitions, and the triggers. The lifecycle is what makes conviction auditable and makes "the facts changed" distinguishable from "I changed the story."

## State 1 — Formation

A Thesis is **formed** when the investor commits an explicit belief to the record. Formation requires, at minimum:

- A **core claim**: the single sentence stating why this Company will succeed as an investment.
- **Load-bearing assumptions**: the small set of things that actually have to be true. These are the Thesis's structural spine.
- **Invalidation conditions**: the observable states of the world that would prove the Thesis wrong. A Thesis cannot leave Formation without at least one, because a Thesis with no invalidation condition is not falsifiable and cannot be tested, tracked, or learned from.
- **Named Risks and Catalysts**: the identified factors that could break the Thesis and the anticipated future events that could confirm or invalidate it.
- **Fit to Capital Mission**: confirmation that the Thesis's implied horizon and risk profile are consistent with the investor's stated Capital Mission.

Formation does not require a Decision. A Thesis may be formed and held in a watch state without a Holding. Formation *does* require that the belief be specific enough to be wrong.

**Trigger into Formation:** the investor articulates a testable belief about a Company, typically prompted by an Observation or by initial Knowledge about the business.

## State 2 — Evidence-Gathering

Once formed, a Thesis enters **evidence-gathering**, where the Investment Case is assembled: the structured, evidence-backed argument built to support or challenge the Thesis at this point in time. Here the load-bearing assumptions are each mapped to Evidence — both confirming and, mandatorily, disconfirming. The framework treats a Case with no disconfirming Evidence as incomplete rather than strong.

During this state the Thesis is provisional. Its Quality Framework profile is computed. The output of this state is a Thesis that is either well-supported enough to underwrite a Decision, or one whose gaps are now explicit.

**Trigger out:** the Investment Case reaches sufficiency (each load-bearing assumption has relevant, sourced Evidence and known disconfirming Evidence has been weighed) — or the investor abandons the Thesis as unsupportable, sending it directly to Retirement.

## State 3 — Active / Under Test

A Thesis becomes **active** when it underwrites a Holding via a Decision, or is held as an active watch-list conviction. This is the steady state of the lifecycle and where AQI's adaptive machinery runs continuously.

In the active state:
- **Observations** about the Company or the world arrive continuously and are evaluated against the Thesis's load-bearing assumptions and invalidation conditions.
- Observations that bear on the Thesis are promoted to **Evidence** and attached to the Investment Case, strengthening or weakening it.
- **Risks** are monitored for materialization; **Catalysts** are tracked toward resolution.
- The Thesis's Quality Framework profile is re-computed as the Evidence base shifts.

An active Thesis is a hypothesis under continuous test, not a settled commitment. Most of a Holding's life is spent here.

**Triggers within Active:** arrival of Evidence that crosses a pre-committed materiality threshold; a named Risk beginning to materialize; a Catalyst approaching or resolving. Each of these can move the Thesis toward Challenge, Confirmation, or Invalidation.

## State 4 — Challenge

**Challenge** is a distinguished, deliberate episode within the active state: a structured re-test of the Thesis, either scheduled (periodic review) or triggered by a material event. In Challenge, the investor argues the Thesis's *opposite* — assembling the strongest disconfirming Investment Case — and re-checks the load-bearing assumptions against current Evidence.

Challenge exists to counteract the strongest behavioral failure in investing: ignoring disconfirming information about a position one already holds. Aegis draws on the Behavior Profile to know when a given investor is most prone to this and to prompt Challenge accordingly.

**Triggers into Challenge:** a scheduled review interval elapsing; a named Risk materializing; a Catalyst resolving against the Thesis; an Observation that contradicts a load-bearing assumption; or a Policy requiring re-test after a defined drawdown.

The outcome of Challenge is one of: **reaffirm** (Thesis survives, return to Active), **revise** (Thesis is narrowed or amended — recorded as a new version alongside the original), or **break** (route to Invalidation).

## State 5 — Confirmation

A Thesis reaches **Confirmation** when its load-bearing assumptions have been borne out by Evidence and its key Catalysts have resolved in its favor. Confirmation is not "the price went up" — it is the reasoning being validated by the world. A confirmed Thesis may continue as an active Holding (the investment thesis played out and the position is held for continued compounding), be resized, or be harvested. Confirmation always produces a **Learning Event** capturing *why the reasoning worked*, feeding Knowledge and the Behavior Profile.

**Trigger:** load-bearing assumptions verified by Evidence and defining Catalysts resolved favorably.

## State 6 — Invalidation

A Thesis reaches **Invalidation** when a load-bearing assumption is disproven or a pre-committed invalidation condition is met. Invalidation is a factual state, not a mood: it is defined by the conditions written at Formation. This design is deliberate — it forces the exit criterion to be set *before* the investor is emotionally committed. An invalidated Thesis mandates a Decision (typically sell or resize) and always produces a Learning Event capturing *why the reasoning failed*.

**Trigger:** an invalidation condition is met, or a load-bearing assumption is disproven by Evidence.

## State 7 — Retirement

A Thesis is **retired** when it is no longer live: it was confirmed and harvested, invalidated and exited, or abandoned before ever underwriting a Decision. Retirement is not deletion. Every retired Thesis — with its full history, versions, Evidence, and outcome — persists as Knowledge. Retired theses are the raw material of institutional memory: they are what the Learning Loop mines to improve future Thesis Formation and to update the Behavior Profile.

**Trigger:** the Thesis's Decision is closed out (position fully exited or watch conviction dropped) and its Learning Event is captured.

## The lifecycle as an audit trail

The value of the lifecycle is not the states in isolation but the *recorded path* through them. Because every transition names its trigger and preserves prior versions, Aegis can always answer: what did the investor believe, when, on what Evidence, and why did that belief change? That auditability is what separates principled adaptation from thesis drift, and it is the substrate on which every other part of AQI's learning machinery operates.
