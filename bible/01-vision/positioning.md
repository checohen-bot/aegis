# Positioning: Apple Simplicity × Palantir Depth

Aegis occupies a position that the market has left vacant because it is hard to hold: an instrument of institutional-grade analytical rigor that an individual can pick up and trust in the first five minutes. We describe this as *Apple-level simplicity, Palantir-level depth*. This document makes that phrase concrete enough to build against, because as a slogan it is inspiring and as a specification it is dangerously vague.

## What Apple-level means here

Apple-level is not minimalism as aesthetic. It is a chain of specific commitments.

**Interface: one honest surface.** At any moment, the screen shows the one thing that matters now and makes the next action obvious. A portfolio is not a grid of blinking tickers; it is a set of theses in various states of health. The default view answers "what deserves my attention?" — not "here is everything we know." Density is never the default. Red and green are earned by thesis-relevant events, not by today's price wiggle.

**Onboarding: value before configuration.** The user connects Interactive Brokers and within minutes sees their own portfolio reflected back as something they have never seen before: holdings with implicit theses waiting to be made explicit, risks unnamed, reasoning unrecorded. We do not open with a questionnaire, a tutorial, or an empty workspace. We open with recognition — *this is your money, and here is what you don't yet know about your own decisions.*

**Trust: the interface never lies, hedges, or performs.** Every number has a source and a timestamp reachable in one interaction. When the system is uncertain, it says so plainly rather than dressing uncertainty in false precision. When it has nothing useful to say, it says nothing — silence is a feature. Trust is also visual: calm typography, no dark patterns, no urgency theater, nothing that moves unless movement means something.

**Restraint: the courage to omit.** Apple-level is defined by what is absent. No day-trading affordances. No gamified streaks. No social feed of other people's positions. No feature shipped because a competitor has it. Every element on screen has survived the question: *does removing this make a decision worse?* If not, it goes.

## What Palantir-level means here

Palantir-level is not complexity as credential. It is the conviction that serious decisions deserve serious machinery — and that the machinery must be *inspectable*.

**Rigor: a real ontology, not a feature pile.** Beneath the surface, Aegis runs on a canonical domain model — Thesis, Evidence, Observation, Decision, Risk, Catalyst, Learning Event, and their kin — with defined relationships and lifecycles. A thesis is not a text note; it is a structured claim with falsification conditions. This ontology is what lets the system reason, and what will let it generalize beyond public equities without a rewrite.

**Evidence graphs: everything connects to why.** Every decision links to the case that justified it; every case links to its evidence; every piece of evidence links to its source, date, and reliability. The user can traverse this graph in both directions: from a holding down to the filing paragraph that anchors it, or from a new observation up to every thesis it threatens. This graph is the product's soul. Screeners have data; Aegis has *structured reasoning about* data.

**Auditability: the past is immutable and reconstructible.** What did I believe on the day I bought, and what did I know? Aegis can answer exactly, because decisions, evidence states, and reasoning are versioned at decision time. This is how institutions learn and individuals don't. We are giving the individual the institutional memory they never had.

**Depth on demand: the full machine is always one deliberate step away.** Nothing is dumbed down or discarded — it is *deferred*. The analyst-grade view of any object exists and is reachable whenever the user wants to go deeper.

## Resolving the tension: progressive disclosure, not dumbing down

Simplicity and depth are only in tension if you believe every capability must be visible to exist. We reject that. The resolution is **progressive disclosure with full depth beneath every surface**:

- **The surface states conclusions.** "This thesis is weakening" — one line, calm, actionable.
- **One step down sits the reasoning.** Which evidence changed, which risk materialized, what the falsification condition said.
- **One step further sits the full graph.** Sources, history, counter-evidence, the complete audit trail.

The critical rule: *every simple statement must be backed by traversable depth, and every deep structure must roll up to a simple statement.* A conclusion with no reasoning underneath is forbidden — that is how chatbots hallucinate authority. Depth with no summary above it is equally forbidden — that is how terminals bury insight. Simplicity that cannot survive interrogation is a lie; depth that cannot be summoned in one sentence is indulgence. Aegis is the discipline of refusing both.

This positioning is also our moat. Companies engineered for engagement cannot adopt our restraint without destroying their business model. Companies built as data terminals cannot achieve our simplicity without rebuilding their foundations. The position is hard to hold — which is precisely why, held well, it will be ours alone.
